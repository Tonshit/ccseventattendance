What This System Does

QR Code Check-In Scan the rotating QR on screen — codes change every 60 seconds, so screenshots can't be shared or reused.
Time In & Time Out Scan once when you arrive, scan again when you leave — both times are recorded automatically.
Your Attendance Record See every event you joined in the My Record tab — records from the last 7 days.
Live Reports & PDFs Organizers watch attendance in real time and export present/absent reports instantly.
Made For Mobile Designed for phones at the door — simple taps, big buttons, vibrations when it works.

How To Use It

1

Set your School ID and name in the Profile tab (one time only).

2

Pick your year level on the Check In tab — it is remembered for next time.

3

Scan the QR (or type the code) when you arrive — that's your time in.

4

Scan again when leaving — that's your time out. Done!
